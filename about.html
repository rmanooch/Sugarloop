Our story

Meet the Baker Behind Sugarloop Co. 🤍

Made with love. Created to celebrate.

Welcome to Sugarloop Co., where every sweet creation comes with a little love, creativity, and a personal touch.

I’m a mom of two wonderful boys and someone who has always loved bringing people together through food and celebrations.

Baking started as something I loved doing for my family and friends. From creating special birthday cakes for my children to making delicious homemade baklava for loved ones, I discovered how much joy I found in turning simple ingredients into beautiful memories.

With encouragement from family and friends, I finally decided to turn that passion into Sugarloop Co.

For me, it’s about more than just baking. It’s about the excitement of a child seeing their birthday cake, the happiness of sharing desserts around a family table, and being part of life’s sweetest moments.

Today, I specialize in custom celebration cakes, cupcakes, and handcrafted pistachio and walnut baklava, combining beautiful designs with delicious flavors.

Every order is made with care, attention to detail, and a whole lot of love.

Thank you for supporting my small business and allowing me to be a little part of your special celebrations.

With love,
The Baker Behind Sugarloop Co. 🤍
